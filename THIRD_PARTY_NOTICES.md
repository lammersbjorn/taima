## Kushy cannabis dataset

Taima's community strain data (`App/Taima/Resources/strains.json`, generated by `scripts/build-strain-db.py`) comes from the [Kushy cannabis dataset](https://github.com/kushyapp/cannabis-dataset), export of 14 November 2017. Taima uses strain names, types, breeders, effects, flavours and terpenes. It leaves out descriptions, ailments, cannabinoid figures and cross references.

## CUDIT-R

The self-check uses the Cannabis Use Disorders Identification Test, Revised: Adamson, S. J., Kay-Lambkin, F. J., Baker, A. L., et al. (2010), *An improved brief measure of cannabis misuse*, Drug and Alcohol Dependence 110(1–2): 137–143. It is distributed as public domain with citation.
