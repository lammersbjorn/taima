# Taima Android trial
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
android

## Stack
Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material 3, explicit SQLite. The maintainer specified this stack and the trial scope.

## Users
Adults who keep a private collection of cannabis bought in the Dutch regulated experiment.

## Product Purpose
Record a collection and stash locally, and test the Android contracts and native-app direction for Saiko Next's planning.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Collection list/detail; manual add/edit; one bag in use; quick amounts and session log; basic statistics; an English/Dutch party JSON preview; format-2 backup and CSV exchange. Synthetic evaluation only. No account, internet permission, analytics, CI, paid services or publication. Label OCR is a research question, not a claimed feature. JSON documents preserve fields outside the trial editor.

## Brand Commitments
The maintainer specified neutral surfaces, green #1B7A47 as the single accent, calm typography and rounded or tabular numbers, and native Android conventions. The existing Taima iOS product is the behavioral reference, not a UI template.

## Evidence on Hand
`../contracts/golden` and `../docs/evidence/android-trial`. Rendered Android acceptance is recorded separately from source and contract checks.
