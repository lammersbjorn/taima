# Taima audit, 27 October 2026 (build 4, 1.3)

Four independent audits of `95ac6f8`: engineering, privacy and accessibility, UX against screenshots of the owner's data, and competitors and references. Findings are merged and ranked here. Measured costs were taken on a Mac with 500 synthetic bags and 5,000 sessions; an iPhone is roughly 2 to 3 times slower.

## Tier 1: fix before adding anything

1. **The Face ID lock doesn't cover sheets.** The lock is an overlay on the root view, so sheets and covers draw above it. A widget, Live Activity, Spotlight or `taima://` link can open a bag on top of the lock, and an open sheet stays visible in the app switcher. Fix: draw the lock in its own `UIWindow` above everything, and hold links until unlocked. (`Privacy.swift`, `TaimaApp.swift`)
2. **Siri and Shortcuts get past the lock and discreet mode.** "How much is in my stash" and "Log usage" run from a locked phone and speak gram amounts and bag names. Fix: require authentication for the Siri intents, and keep names out of dialogs in discreet mode. (`Shortcuts.swift`, `TaimaActivity.swift`)
3. **Stats stutters.** Session insights are recomputed about 23 times per redraw (about 250 ms per frame on a Mac at 500 bags), so dragging a chart stutters. Fix: compute the summaries once per data change and pass them down. (`StatsView.swift`)
4. **Backups run out of memory.** One JSON file with base64 photos reaches about 335 MB and a 1.7 GB peak at 500 bags, so iOS likely kills the app. The backup also misses remembered growers. Fix: a folder or zip with JSON plus photo files, built off the main thread; include `producer_key`. (`AppModel.backupFile`, `restore`)
5. **Text contrast fails.** White on `leafMid` is about 2.5:1: selected tags, moods, methods and the Live Activity buttons. Fix: fill with the accent `#1B7A47` (5.4:1). (`Theme.swift` and callers)
6. **Unreadable bags disappear silently.** A downgrade that doesn't know a new enum case (for example `hash`) hides those bags, and the next export drops them for good. Fix: decode unknown enum values leniently and report any rows that fail. (`BagStore.allBags`)
7. **Edits can delete photos without saving.** Photos removed in the editor are deleted before the database write succeeds. Fix: delete them after the save. (`AppModel.save`)

## Tier 2: privacy and accessibility polish

- Discreet mode still shows "Smoked one", smoke and leaf icons and "Used today" on the Lock Screen. Use neutral wording and icons.
- Spotlight should skip indexing when the Face ID lock is on, or use a `.complete` protection class.
- Leaflet links read from labels open without confirmation. Allow https only, ask first, and validate the PRD before building the Potspot URL.
- Offer encrypted backups (passphrase, AES-GCM), on by default. Offer to exclude the data from iCloud device backup. The CSV export should warn that it's plain text and neutralise formula cells.
- Redact the App Group snapshot and delete the thumbnail at write time in discreet mode.
- Photos: use `.complete` file protection, re-encode on restore, and copy with `localOnly` and an expiry.
- Charts: use selection VoiceOver can operate (no tap-only overlays), add chart descriptors, and give tappable rows the button trait.
- Dynamic Type: no fixed point sizes; FlowLayout should wrap long tags; MoodRow and the family tree need layouts for accessibility sizes. Labels need context ("Mood before, Low").
- Touch targets of at least 44 pt for chips and nodes. Respect Reduce Motion in toasts and rings.

## Tier 3: UX restructure

- **Stash repeats itself.** The same grams appear in the header, the in-use ring and the bottom bar. Merge the header and in-use cards, and hide the bottom accessory on Stash.
- **Three search paths.** Collection search, the Bags/Strains switch and the Search tab overlap. Keep the Search tab (bags and library) and turn Collection's controls into filters and sorting.
- **Collection repeats Stats.** Its summary strip repeats the Stats hero; drop the strip.
- **Navigation.** Settings is reachable from every tab, not only Collection. The scanner moves into the + menu as "Check a bag in the shop". Share sits in the detail toolbar. The detail gets a "Strain" row and a family tree preview.
- **Logging.** For the bag in use, "Log a session" sits near the top of the detail.
- **Empty states.** The Search screen shows recent bags, the wishlist and suggestions, with the mascot in empty states.
- **Tools.** The countdown becomes a small row and icons stay in one green hue. Stash age and the tolerance break could become Stats insights.
- **Light mode.** Cards get a tinted fill or hairline; one card radius, padding and shadow rule in `Theme`.
- **Small fixes.**
  - Dutch decimal commas in the bottom bar.
  - Milestone cells with a fixed two-line height.
  - The glow background on Search.
  - One-line titles above the bottom bar.

## Tier 4: features from competitors and references

- **Automatic rotating backups** to a Files folder (Beanconqueror). SideStore expiry or a reinstall is the biggest data-loss risk.
- **Harm reduction from Trimbos, bundled offline with sources.**
  - A tips card.
  - A "mixed with tobacco" toggle per session, with its share in Stats.
  - An edible wait timer as a Live Activity (30 to 90 minutes).
- **An optional private CUDIT-R self-check every few months.** Neutral wording at the thresholds and links to drugsinfo.nl (0900-1995). Check the instrument's licence before bundling it.
- **Use-free days per week** next to the weekly limit; tolerance breaks show grams and euros not used, plus a history of breaks.
- **Freshness.** "Use first" ordering by pack date in Stash.
- **Would buy again.** A flag shown in the shop scanner.
- **Correlations.** Mood change against THC, CBD, method, time and grower, with session counts and no claims of cause (Bearable, Releaf).
- **Year in review.** A share card that follows the existing privacy rules.
- **CSV import** and a documented data format.

Ruled out: cloud sync, community reviews, any track-and-trace lookup (offline and privacy), Apple Health State of Mind (HealthKit entitlement on a free Apple ID unconfirmed; Health syncs to iCloud).

## Engineering follow-ups

- **SQLite.** Add `busy_timeout` and `BEGIN IMMEDIATE`; `bag(id:)` should use `WHERE id = ?`; build the summary once per Live Activity tap (about 150 ms now).
- **Shared code.** `InventoryActions.use` and `AppModel.use` should share one implementation.
- **Main thread.** Load the strain catalog in the background at launch (241 ms on the main thread now).
- **Widget snapshot writes.** Serialise them. Rebuild Spotlight incrementally, not from scratch on every save.
- **Time zone.** `Calendar.taima` should follow time zone changes (`.autoupdatingCurrent`).
- **AppModel (788 lines).** Move the editor merge, rescan field preservation and restore into tested TaimaCore functions.
- **Tests for:**
  - a full-field backup round trip;
  - migration from schema 0 and 1;
  - photo deletion on save, cancel and delete;
  - restore path traversal;
  - `WidgetSnapshot`;
  - `InventoryActions`.
- **Release.**
  - Mark IPAs built from a dirty tree.
  - Keep dSYMs.
  - Derive or check the build number, since What's New keys on it.
